Ayah Reminder – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Ayah Reminder ("the app") shows a short Quran Ayah card when you unlock your phone. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: the app does not collect your personal information and does not send your reading data to the developer.

1. Information stored on your device

Your settings, reading progress, favourites, notes, collections, daily goal and statistics are stored only on your device. We do not receive them. If you uninstall the app, this data is removed from your device unless you saved a backup file yourself.

2. Backups

The backup feature creates a file that you choose where to save. The developer does not receive or store this file.

3. Payments

Subscriptions are processed by Google Play. Google handles your payment details and billing. We do not receive your card, UPI or bank details. Google's own privacy policy applies to payments.

4. Permissions

  • Display over other apps: used only to show the Ayah card after you unlock your phone.
  • Foreground service and notification: a small ongoing notification shows that the reminder is active.
  • Start at boot: restarts the reminder after your phone restarts, if you turned it on.

The app does not read the content of other apps, your messages, contacts, call logs, photos or location. It does not record which apps you open.

5. Analytics, ads and sharing

The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools. We do not sell or share your personal information.

6. Quran content

The Arabic Quran text is from the Tanzil Project (tanzil.net), licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0, and is reproduced without changes. English and Hindi translations are machine-generated by an AI model in advance and included inside the app. They may contain errors and are not a substitute for scholarly translations or religious guidance. The app does not send your data to any AI service.

7. Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
